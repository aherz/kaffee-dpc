These checkpoints are probabilistic adaptations of the DynaMix base weights
by Christoph Jürgen Hemmer and Daniel Durstewitz. We fitted stochastic shells
and, where specified by the baseline, updated the dynamical core.
Source of the base weights: https://huggingface.co/DurstewitzLab/dynamix
